  • • Cluster Extraction: Parallel GB10 extraction across spark1 (10,000) & spark2 (10,022)

  • • Interconnect: Dual 200Gbps Direct Attach Copper (400Gbps aggregate)

3. Dual Objective Loss Formulation

Total Loss: $\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{recon}} + 0.25 \times \mathcal{L}_{\text{conf}}$

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{recon}}$: Cosine Similarity + MSE against Bonsai 2 Layer 62 representation (aligning drafter latent space with target model)

$\mathcal{L}_{\text{conf}}$: Binary Cross-Entropy on $\text{BCEWithLogits}(\text{conf\_logits}, \mathbb{I}(\text{cos\_sim} \ge 0.85))$

Goal: Confidently emit 5 tokens per step with $\ge 95\%$ acceptance

Runtime Bug Resolution: The Hadamard Fix Resolved & Verified

During initial testing against Bonsai 2, acceptance collapsed to 0.89% (13.2 tok/s, -53% degradation).

Root Cause: PrismML's ternary base model applies Hadamard rotations. In llama.cpp's DFlash runtime:

  • tok_embd was missing the inverse Hadamard transform
  • output.weight was not inheriting hadamard_rotations from ctx_other

Result: After patching, acceptance jumped from 0.89% to 69.23%, and throughput surged from 13.20 tok/s to 47.85 tok/s (+72.2% over baseline).
